## 1. Architectural Pattern & Design Philosophy
ODtech ERP follows a modular **Django "Monolith-First" Service Layer Architecture**:
- **Thin Views:** Views handle HTTP requests, role authentication checks, and call domain services or return JSON/HTML.
- **Rich Service Layer:** Complex business logic (Tax calculations, Document generation, EDMS permissions, Stock movements) resides in dedicated `services/` modules (e.g. `documents/services.py`, `edms/services/`, `inventory/services.py`).
- **Reactive Signals:** Cross-module synchronization is decoupled via Django signals (e.g., commercial documents automatically sync to EDMS records via `documents/signals.py`, order updates log audit entries via `tracker/signals.py`).

## 3. Core Subsystems & Data Flows

### A. Order Tracker & Manufacturing Flow (`tracker`)
1. **Order Creation:** Orders enter with customer, reference, and delivery milestones.
2. **Lot Splitting:** Large orders are split into manufacturing Lots (e.g., Lot A, Lot B) with independent product lists and quantities.
3. **Supplier Costing & Price Approvals:** Cost options are attached to order lines. Discrepancies trigger a `PriceApprovalRequest` workflow.
4. **Dispatch:** Dispatches generate Challans and link to commercial documents.

### B. Commercial Documents (`documents`)
1. Multi-document engine supporting:
   - Quotation (`QTN`)
   - Proforma Invoice (`PRO`)
   - Tax Invoice (`INV`)
   - Delivery Challan (`CHL`)
   - Purchase Order (`PO`)
   - Credit Note (`CRN`) / Debit Note (`DBN`)
2. Auto-synchronization to EDMS:
   - When commercial documents are saved, `documents/signals.py` updates or creates an `EDMSDocument` mirror for central document auditing and global search.

### C. Enterprise Document Management System (`edms`)
- Granular permission matrix per document category and department.
- Supports PDF preview, OCR text extraction, OTP-protected downloads, and audit logs.
- Vendor invoice repository tracking raw material purchase invoices.

### D. Financial & Executive Reporting (`reporting`)
- Executive Company Overview Dashboard aggregating:
  - Total Sales (from approved Invoices)
  - Total Purchases (from EDMS purchase invoices & vendor bills)
  - Gross & Net Profit margins
  - Total PIs Generated (tracking conversion to Tax Invoices)
  - Receivables & Payables aging
